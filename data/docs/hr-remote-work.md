# Contoso Remote Work Policy

**Policy ID:** HR-REMOTE-002  
**Effective date:** 1 January 2026  
**Policy owner:** Human Resources

## 1. Purpose and scope

This policy outlines guidelines, eligibility criteria, and expectations for employees working remotely or in a hybrid arrangement at Contoso. It is a fictional policy created for testing the Contoso PolicyAssistant project and does not represent a real company's rules.

## 2. Eligibility and hybrid schedules

Full-time employees whose roles do not require continuous on-site presence may request a hybrid schedule after completing their initial 90-day probationary period. Standard hybrid arrangements allow up to two remote days per week, subject to direct manager approval and team coverage requirements.

Fully remote arrangements require documented business justification, approval from the department director, and formal sign-off from Human Resources.

## 3. Workspace and environment requirements

Remote employees are responsible for maintaining a dedicated, quiet, and ergonomically safe workspace. The remote work area must allow for secure phone and video conversations without risk of exposing proprietary or customer information.

Contoso does not cover personal utility costs, home internet upgrades, or home office renovation expenses unless mandated by local employment regulations.

## 4. Availability and core working hours

Remote employees must remain reachable via official Contoso communication channels (Teams and email) during standard core business hours (9:00 AM to 3:00 PM local time). Expected response times during core hours mirror those of in-office employees.

Any temporary unavailability during scheduled working hours must be coordinated with the direct manager in advance.

## 5. Equipment and information security

Company-issued laptops and accessories remain Contoso property and must only be used by authorized employees for business purposes. Remote workers must connect via the corporate VPN when accessing internal networks and ensure their home Wi-Fi networks use WPA2 or WPA3 encryption.

Unattended devices must be locked, and confidential hard-copy documents should not be printed at home without explicit written consent.

## 6. Responsibilities

Employees are responsible for fulfilling performance objectives, maintaining professional availability, and protecting company assets. Managers are responsible for monitoring performance, ensuring equitable team communication, and scheduling regular check-ins. Human Resources oversees remote working agreements and policy compliance.

## 7. Questions and exceptions

Questions about this policy should be directed to Human Resources. Any permanent exception or out-of-region remote work arrangement requires written approval from the Head of Human Resources.
