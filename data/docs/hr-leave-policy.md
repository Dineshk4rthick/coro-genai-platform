# Contoso Employee Leave Policy

**Policy ID:** HR-LEAVE-001  
**Effective date:** 1 January 2026  
**Policy owner:** Human Resources

## 1. Purpose and scope

This policy explains the leave entitlements and approval process for full-time Contoso employees. It is a fictional policy created for testing the Contoso PolicyAssistant project and does not represent a real company's rules.

## 2. Annual leave

Eligible full-time employees receive 20 working days of annual leave per calendar year. Employees joining after 1 January receive a prorated entitlement based on their completed months of service. Annual leave is measured in working days, excluding weekends and company holidays.

Employees should submit annual leave requests at least five working days before the intended start date. Their direct manager must approve the request before the leave begins. A request is not approved merely because it has been submitted.

## 3. Sick leave

Eligible full-time employees receive 10 working days of sick leave per calendar year. If an employee is absent due to illness for more than two consecutive working days, they must provide a medical certificate to Human Resources within five working days of returning to work.

Employees should notify their direct manager as soon as reasonably possible when illness prevents them from working. Sick leave records are maintained by Human Resources.

## 4. Emergency leave

Employees may request up to three working days of emergency leave for an unexpected personal emergency. They must notify their manager as soon as possible and submit the leave request through the normal leave process when they are able to do so.

Emergency leave approval is subject to manager review. It is not automatically granted solely because the request is marked as an emergency.

## 5. Leave balances and records

Employees can request their current leave balance from Human Resources. Managers are responsible for reviewing requests and ensuring that team coverage is considered before approving annual leave.

Employees must not submit inaccurate leave dates or claim leave that they did not take. Suspected discrepancies should be reported to Human Resources for review.

## 6. Responsibilities

Employees are responsible for submitting accurate requests and checking approval status. Direct managers are responsible for reviewing requests and communicating decisions. Human Resources maintains leave records and answers questions about entitlements and policy interpretation.

## 7. Questions and exceptions

Questions about this policy should be directed to Human Resources. Any exception to the stated entitlements requires written approval from the Head of Human Resources.
