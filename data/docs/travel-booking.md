# Contoso Corporate Travel Booking Policy

**Policy ID:** TRV-BOOK-001  
**Effective date:** 1 January 2026  
**Policy owner:** Finance & Travel Operations

## 1. Purpose and scope

This policy governs the booking of business travel, including flights, rail travel, hotels, and vehicle rentals for Contoso employees traveling on authorized company business. It is a fictional policy created for testing the Contoso PolicyAssistant project and does not represent a real company's rules.

## 2. Pre-approval requirements

All business travel must be formally pre-approved before any bookings are finalized. Employees must submit a travel pre-authorization request outlining the business justification, expected destination, and estimated cost:
- Domestic trips require direct manager approval at least 14 days in advance.
- International trips require direct manager approval and department head approval at least 28 days in advance.

Bookings made without prior written authorization will not be reimbursed.

## 3. Booking channel and preferred vendors

All travel arrangements must be booked using Contoso's centralized Travel Management Portal (TMP). Bookings made through consumer websites or personal reward platforms are non-compliant and may be rejected unless the TMP was unavailable and prior travel desk approval was granted.

Employees must select designated corporate preferred vendors for airlines, hotels, and car rentals to ensure negotiated corporate rates and duty-of-care tracking apply.

## 4. Travel class and accommodation standards

Travel bookings must adhere to the following class guidelines:
- **Air travel:** Economy class is standard for all domestic and short-haul flights. Premium Economy may be booked for continuous flight segments exceeding eight hours. Business class requires executive approval.
- **Rail travel:** Standard/Economy class is standard. First class is permitted only when standard seating is sold out and travel is time-critical.
- **Hotel lodging:** Standard single rooms at approved business-tier hotels. Nightly rates must not exceed regional caps specified in the portal (e.g., $220/night in major metropolitan centers).

## 5. Itinerary changes and cancellations

Changes and cancellations must be made directly through the Travel Management Portal. Employees must cancel reservations as early as possible to minimize penalty fees.

Personal modifications or extended stays added onto a business trip must be paid separately by the employee at their own expense.

## 6. Responsibilities

Traveling employees are responsible for adhering to booking timelines, staying within policy thresholds, and maintaining their travel profile details. Managers are responsible for validating the business necessity and budget of the travel. The Travel Operations team oversees booking portal support and vendor partnerships.

## 7. Questions and exceptions

Questions regarding booking procedures or travel portal access should be directed to Travel Operations. Exceptions to cabin class or rate caps require written approval from the Head of Finance.
