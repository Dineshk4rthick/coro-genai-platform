# Contoso Travel and Entertainment Expense Policy

**Policy ID:** TRV-EXP-002  
**Effective date:** 1 January 2026  
**Policy owner:** Finance & Accounting

## 1. Purpose and scope

This policy outlines the rules, limits, and submission processes for reimbursing expenses incurred by employees during authorized Contoso business travel and client entertainment. It is a fictional policy created for testing the Contoso PolicyAssistant project and does not represent a real company's rules.

## 2. Daily meal allowances (per diem)

Employees on overnight business travel are eligible for meal expense reimbursement up to the standard daily cap:
- Standard domestic rate: Up to $75 per full travel day (breakfast: $15, lunch: $25, dinner: $35).
- International rate: Up to $100 per full travel day (adjusted by destination tier in the expense portal).

Alcohol purchases are not eligible for reimbursement under standard individual per diem meals. Meal allowances cannot be carried over or pooled across multiple days.

## 3. Ground transportation

Employees should select the most economical practical ground transportation option. Permissible ground transport includes:
- Airport shuttles, public transit, and standard rideshares (e.g., UberX, Lyft standard).
- Personal vehicle mileage for business use, reimbursed at the standard statutory rate per mile; fuel for personal cars is covered by the mileage rate and cannot be claimed separately.
- Rental cars are authorized only when public transit or ridesharing is impractical or more costly for group travel.

Traffic citations, parking tickets, and vehicle towing fees are the sole responsibility of the employee and will not be reimbursed.

## 4. Client entertainment and meals

Business meals with clients or external partners must have a clear business objective. The expense claim must itemize:
- The full names, titles, and company affiliations of all attendees.
- The specific business topic discussed.
- An itemized receipt showing all food and beverage charges.

The most senior Contoso employee in attendance must pay for the meal and submit the claim.

## 5. Submission deadlines and receipts

Expense reports must be submitted through the corporate expense software within 30 calendar days of returning from travel. 

Original itemized receipts are mandatory for any individual expense exceeding $25. Credit card summary slips that do not display individual line items are insufficient. Claims submitted after 60 calendar days will be automatically rejected.

## 6. Responsibilities

Employees are responsible for submitting honest, itemized expense reports with valid documentation. Managers are responsible for reviewing expense claims for reasonableness and policy compliance before approval. Finance audits submissions and processes reimbursements.

## 7. Questions and exceptions

Questions about expense eligibility or report status should be directed to the Finance & Accounting Department. Any exception to reimbursement limits requires written approval from the Head of Finance.
