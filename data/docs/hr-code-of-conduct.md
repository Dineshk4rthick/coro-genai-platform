# Contoso Code of Conduct

**Policy ID:** HR-CONDUCT-003  
**Effective date:** 1 January 2026  
**Policy owner:** Human Resources

## 1. Purpose and scope

This policy establishes the standards of professional behavior, integrity, and ethical conduct expected of all Contoso employees, contractors, and representatives. It is a fictional policy created for testing the Contoso PolicyAssistant project and does not represent a real company's rules.

## 2. Workplace respect and anti-harassment

Contoso maintains a strict zero-tolerance stance toward discrimination, harassment, bullying, and retaliation. All employees are entitled to a safe, inclusive, and professional environment regardless of race, gender, age, religion, sexual orientation, disability, or national origin.

Offensive remarks, unwanted physical contact, intimidation, or derogatory humor are strictly prohibited across all Contoso offices, events, and digital communication platforms.

## 3. Conflicts of interest

Employees must avoid situations where personal, financial, or familial interests conflict with Contoso’s operational or commercial interests. Any outside employment, board membership, or secondary commercial engagement must be disclosed in writing to Human Resources and the employee’s department head prior to commencement.

Acceptance of supplier or vendor gifts exceeding $50 in estimated value is prohibited without written disclosure and compliance approval.

## 4. Confidentiality and company assets

Employees are entrusted with sensitive business information, customer data, and intellectual property. Unauthorized sharing, public disclosure, or copying of non-public Contoso data—even after separation of employment—is a violation of this code and subject to legal remedy.

Company physical assets, intellectual property, and communication systems must be protected against theft, damage, or unauthorized third-party access.

## 5. Reporting misconduct and non-retaliation

Employees who observe or experience violations of this policy are encouraged to report the incident immediately to their direct manager, Human Resources, or via the anonymous Contoso Ethics Hotline.

Contoso strictly prohibits retaliation against any employee who reports suspected misconduct in good faith or participates in an investigation.

## 6. Responsibilities

All employees must understand and adhere to these ethical standards in daily operations. Managers must model ethical behavior, address early warning signs of misconduct, and forward complaints promptly to Human Resources. Human Resources is responsible for conducting fair, confidential investigations and enforcing corrective measures.

## 7. Questions and exceptions

Questions regarding ethical scenarios or potential conflicts of interest should be directed to Human Resources or the Compliance Office. Waivers to this policy are granted only in extraordinary circumstances and require written approval from the Head of Human Resources.
