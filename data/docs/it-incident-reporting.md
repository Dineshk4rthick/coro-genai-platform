# Contoso IT Security Incident Reporting Policy

**Policy ID:** IT-INC-003  
**Effective date:** 1 January 2026  
**Policy owner:** Information Technology & Security

## 1. Purpose and scope

This policy defines the procedure for identifying, reporting, and managing suspected or confirmed information security incidents affecting Contoso assets. It is a fictional policy created for testing the Contoso PolicyAssistant project and does not represent a real company's rules.

## 2. Definition of an incident

A security incident is any adverse event that compromises the confidentiality, integrity, or availability of Contoso data, systems, or physical infrastructure. Examples include:
- Suspected phishing emails clicked by an employee.
- Lost, stolen, or compromised company laptops, phones, or security badges.
- Ransomware warnings, unexpected system lockouts, or severe malware alerts.
- Unauthorized access or accidental exposure of sensitive customer or employee data.

## 3. Immediate reporting procedure

Employees must report any suspected security incident within one hour of discovery:
1. Contact the IT Security Incident Response Team immediately via the dedicated email address (`security-alert@contoso.local`) or phone hotline.
2. In the event of an active ransomware alert or erratic machine behavior, immediately disconnect the device from Wi-Fi and unplug the Ethernet cable, but do not power off the device.
3. Provide a clear summary of what occurred, including error messages, affected systems, and timestamps.

## 4. Phishing email reporting

Suspicious emails should not be forwarded to individual colleagues. Instead, employees must use the "Report Phishing" button embedded in their email client. If the button is unavailable, forward the message as an attachment to `phishing-report@contoso.local` and delete the email from the inbox.

## 5. Containment, investigation, and communication

The IT Security Incident Response Team is solely responsible for leading technical containment, forensics, and remediation activities.

Employees must not independently investigate, attempt system repairs, or discuss ongoing incidents with external third parties, media outlets, or public forums. All public disclosures must be coordinated through corporate communications and legal counsel.

## 6. Responsibilities

All staff members are responsible for remaining vigilant and reporting anomalies promptly without delay. The IT Incident Response Team manages containment, forensic analysis, and post-incident reporting. Management is responsible for facilitating required remediation actions within their teams.

## 7. Questions and exceptions

Questions regarding incident handling should be directed to the IT Security Office. Any deviation from incident response protocols must receive written approval from the Head of IT Security.
